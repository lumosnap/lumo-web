Design decisions: LumoSnap share gallery

A photo that has not arrived yet looks like a photo still loading, not like a broken page.

The tile keeps the grey surface or the ThumbHash it already shows while it loads. We rejected a new "missing" icon, because an album that is still uploading is normal, not an error.

Brief: a client opens the album link while the photographer still uploads

  • Character: the photographer's contact sheet, handed over before every print is dry.
  • For: the couple and their family, who got the album link from the photographer.
  • Job: look through the photos and heart the ones they want.
  • Moment: relaxed browsing, often on a phone, often soon after the event, while the upload can still run.
  • Feel: calm and finished; the studio looks careful even when some photos are not there yet.
The rest of the brief
  • Who sets the look: Existing, Minimal. The share page has a measured design language (canvas, card, inset tokens in app/assets/css/main.css).
  • Scope: control: the photo image element in the grid tile and in the notes panel header.
  • Screens: 375 px to 1440 px and up: phones and desktop. Source: the code (grid-cols-2 on phones, md and xl breakpoints).
  • Genre: photo proofing gallery. Kept conventions: grid of tiles, a grey frame while a photo loads, no error noise per tile.
  • Richness: very low for this control: the photos are the content, so a missing photo must take no attention.
  • Identity: existing.
  • Core copy: no new strings. The file name stays the image alt text for screen readers.
  • Signature moment: hearting a photo; not changed here.
  • Delight piece: none, because a fallback must be invisible.
  • Constraints: no new dependency; one shared fix, not a patch per usage.

The page already has a calm "not loaded yet" surface. So the fix reuses it →

Direction: grey paper, white prints, kept

  • Chosen: the existing direction (grey canvas, white cards, photos as prints). A missing photo is an empty print slot: the inset grey (--color-inset-2) or its ThumbHash, the same surface a tile shows while it loads.

Session 2026-11-02: one shared fallback for every photo element

Clients saw the browser's broken-image icon on photos that were still uploading. One directive, v-img-fallback, now makes a failed image see-through, so the surface under it shows.
