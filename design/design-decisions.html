Design decisions: LumoSnap client gallery

The client gallery now looks like the photographer's app: grey paper, white prints, one blue, and a red heart that is the client's own.

The old page used slate, indigo, glass and a serif title. It looked like a different, older product. Now the album card, the photo sheet, the folder chips and the lightbox use the desktop app's values. Nothing the client does works in a different way.

Brief: a family member on a phone, in the evening, looking for photos of a person they love

  • Character: the family album passed around after the wedding, where everyone first looks for their own face and then for Nani's
  • For: the couple and their family: parents, siblings, grandparents. Most use a phone at 390 px.
  • Job: find photos of me, my mum, or us together in 5,000 to 10,000 photos, then heart the good ones.
  • Moment: relaxed but eager, scrolling a very large album. Not a technical audience.
  • Feel: calm and uncluttered. The faces are theirs; the interface stays quiet.
The rest of the brief
  • Who sets the look: Existing, at Revamp: the slate and indigo look stays; the structure of the filters is open.
  • Scope: flow: people in the v2 gallery (filter row, search, summary line, empty state, lightbox). The v1 gallery is not changed.
  • Screens: 360 px to 2560 px, phones first. Source: the code (viewport meta, md breakpoints) and the requester.
  • Genre: photo gallery. Conventions kept: faces as circles with a name under them (Apple Photos, Google Photos), search with chips, a lightbox.
  • Richness: low. The photos are the content; the filters must use few rows on a phone.
  • Identity: existing: slate greys, indigo 600 for "on", round pills, a serif italic album title.
  • Core copy: "Anjali and Nani · 812 photos", "Together", "Either", "No photos of Rahul and Meera together in Ceremony", "Show Rahul or Meera · 1125".
  • First value: one tap on a face shows that person's photos. No typing.
  • Signature moment: the tap on a face. It gets the largest target and the clearest selected state.
  • Delight piece: none, because the photos already carry the feeling; the interface should not compete.
  • Ending: the client hearts a photo. A face in the lightbox leads to more photos of that person.
  • Flow shape: one page. Filters apply at once; there are no steps.
  • Map: an album has sets (chapters), people (named faces) and tags; a photo can be in many of each. Filters AND across kinds. Every flow hands off to the lightbox, where a face leads back to the grid.
  • Fixed: Nuxt, Tailwind, PhotoSwipe, the manifest format, URL state (?people=, ?q=, ?tags=).
  • Constraints: do not add a stacked row on a phone. SSR output must match the client output.

Direction: a row of faces apart from the tags, not people as a tab, because who and what are different questions

We tried 3 directions on the same 7 people and 8 tags, inside the existing look.

  • A, chosen, faces row: 56 px circles with the first name under them, above the search. A different shape and place from the tags.
  • B, People tab: a "People" item in the set tabs. Lost because sets are chapters of the day; a person is in every chapter. A tab would hide the sets.
  • C, people only in search: type a name to filter. Lost because the family does not know which names the photographer used. Kept as a second way in.
Direction details
  • Default to beat: people as pills in the chip row, with the same shape and the same selected colour as tags.
  • Chosen: A, because a face is found faster than a word, and the row answers "who" before "what".
  • Motif: the circle. Faces are round; tags and sets stay pills.
  • Placements: the faces row, the dashed ring when a typed name matches, and the pills in the lightbox.
  • Style tile: the first 390 px capture was the tile. It showed that names truncate, so the row shows first names.

Session 2026-11-01 (more like this): find photos by how they look: "More like this" from any photo, and search words that find photos the tags missed

Existing work · Revamp inside the given look · phase 4 v1.5: album photo vectors (LSV1), a worker, no model in the browser · selection lists removed

The desktop app now uploads one small file per album: a 768-number fingerprint of each photo. The gallery reads it in a background worker. From any photo, "More like this" shows the closest photos first. A search word first finds the tagged photos, then the photos that look like the word. Albums without the file work as before. The phone card is smaller: the first photo starts at 300 px, not 422 px.

Brief a relative who remembers a photo, not its tag

  • For: the family, in a phone browser or at a desktop browser (both matter; there is no app).
  • Job: "there was one of grandma near the lamp, show me more like that"; "stage", "temple", "kalyanam".
  • Moment: browsing for fun, or looking for one photo. A tag that missed must not mean "no photos".
  • Feel: the gallery understands the photo, quietly. No "AI" word, no score numbers.
  • Signature moment: the tap on "More like this" in the lightbox, and the grid that answers it.
  • Screens: 360 px to 1440 px and more. Source: the project and the owner (phone browsers and desktop browsers are equal).
  • Real content: the LumoTest album (15 photos, ids 24–38) with its real LSV1 file; a synthetic 10,000-photo file (clustered by moment and burst, 7.7 MB).

Direction the photographer's contact sheet, sorted by eye

  • Kept direction: one studio, two rooms. No new colour. One new shape: the square print in the filter bar (round means a person, so a photo is square).
  • Where it shows: (1) a labelled pill in the lightbox, in the row with the faces; (2) one grid header that says why the order is not by time; (3) a "Looks like" header under the tagged matches, so the less sure photos are said to be less sure.
  • Rejected: mixing meaning results into the moments (a family member cannot tell sure from unsure); an icon-only button in the top bar (no room at 390 px with a label, and an icon did not say what it does); a long-press menu on tiles (a long press on a link opens the browser's own menu on phones).

Session 2026-11-01 (selection): the family picks the album: a count under the thumb, chapter limits on the tabs, one photo at a time, then send

Removed later the same day (see the next session up): selection lists removed: clients only give feedback; photographers export via Copy to folder. Its captures are deleted.

The photographer can now ask for lists ("Main album: 300, 40 from Haldi"). With lists, the heart picks into the active list. The count stays at the bottom of the screen. A full chapter stops a pick before anything is sent. "One by one" shows the photos that are not decided yet. "Send" shows a summary, then locks the list. Albums without lists work as before.

Brief a parent with a deadline and 10,000 photos

  • For: the bride or a parent, on a phone, over several evenings; the phone goes round the family.
  • Job: pick the photos for each list the photographer asked for, inside the limits, and send them.
  • Moment: a big album, a date, mild pressure. They want to know "how many more?" and "am I done?".
  • Feel: calm and sure. The count is always true; nothing is lost; a refusal says why at once.
  • Richness: low chrome, as before: the photos are the content. Care goes into the count, the refusals and the ending.
  • Signature moment: Keep in "One by one", and the count that moves with it.
  • Ending: "Sent to Mock Studio": a check, the count, the date, and the next list if one is open.
  • Screens: 360 px to 1440 px and more, phones first. Source: the project and the brief.
  • Real content: 10,000 photos; lists of 300, 60 and 3; chapters of 30, 40, 80; a limit on a chapter that is still uploading (left out); a deadline 2.4 days away, one that passed, one sent list.

Direction the photographer's pick sheet, in the family's hand

  • Kept direction: one studio, two rooms (the desktop look). Selection adds no new colour and no new shape.
  • Where it shows: (1) the count lives in a pill at the bottom, the same shape as the filter bar, under the thumb at any depth; (2) chapter progress lives where chapters already are: the set tabs on phones, a list in the column on wide screens; (3) one by one uses the lightbox's night stage, so it reads as "looking at one photo", not as a new app.
  • Rejected: a cart drawer (shopping, wrong for a family album); a progress header fixed at the top (a third sticky row over the photos on a phone); a separate "selection" page (the person loses the place in 10,000 photos).

Removed

The 1 px line between people and tags. The always-there count line. The "Clear" link on the summary line.
